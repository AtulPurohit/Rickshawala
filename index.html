90s Hindi Bangers & Nostalgia Rides

RICKSHAWALA.FUN is a nostalgic ride through the unforgettable Hindi songs of the 90s — the bangers that travelled with us through Indian streets, auto-rickshaws and old memories.

Listen to a curated collection of unforgettable Hindi songs from the 1990s — the Bollywood bangers, romantic classics, dance tracks and nostalgic songs that defined a generation.

Why Rickshawala?

Remember those auto-rickshaw rides?

The meter running. The city passing by. And somewhere above the sound of traffic, a 90s Hindi banger playing from the Rickshawala's music system.

No algorithm.
No recommendation engine.
Just a great song playing at full volume.

RICKSHAWALA.FUN was created to bring back that feeling.

It is a nostalgic digital ride through the unforgettable Hindi songs of the 90s — created for everyone who still remembers the music, the streets and those little journeys.

About RICKSHAWALA

RICKSHAWALA.FUN is an independent creative project created and developed by Atul Purohit for lovers of 90s Hindi music and the nostalgia of Indian auto-rickshaw culture.

The Ride Back to the 90s

Before playlists lived in our phones, songs travelled with us.

A rickshaw ride across an Indian city could come with its own soundtrack — a cassette, a radio, a crackling speaker and a 90s Hindi banger playing louder than the traffic.

RICKSHAWALA.FUN is a digital tribute to those rides.

A curated collection of 90s Hindi music for the people who still remember the songs, the streets and the feeling.
